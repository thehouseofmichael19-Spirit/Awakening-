Welcome to The House of Michael

Welcome to The House of Michael, a Christian house of faith centered upon the Holy Scriptures and the living presence of God.

Our faith emphasizes the identity and work of the Holy Spirit and seeks to understand God through the words and teachings recorded in Scripture.

Our Faith

We believe that the Holy Spirit is God and that the presence of the Spirit is revealed throughout Scripture.

We look especially to the baptism of Jesus, where the Holy Spirit descends upon Him, and to the words of Jesus concerning the Father, the Spirit, and the relationship revealed between them.

Our Confession

We confess one God and seek to worship God in Spirit and in truth.

The Scriptures testify concerning the Holy Spirit, His presence, His work, and His divine identity. We therefore seek to know God through the revelation given in the Holy Scriptures.

"God is a Spirit: and they that worship him must worship him in spirit and in truth."

— John 4:24
